Do I need to change my current website?

No. Your landing page lives on its own link and your ads point to it. Your current site stays exactly as it is.

Do you only work with dentists?

No. We build pages for dental, medical and other healthcare practices that run Google Ads.

Who writes the copy?

We do, based on your services, your offer and your reviews. You approve everything before it goes live.

Will it work with my Google Ads?

Yes. You point your ad to the new page, and we set up call and form tracking so you can see what your clicks turn into.

How does a better page improve my ROI?

Your ad spend stays the same, but more of those clicks turn into calls and bookings. That lowers what you pay for each new patient and raises the return on every campaign. That's what CRO is about: getting more from the traffic you already pay for.

What about patient privacy and HIPAA?

Our forms only ask for basic contact details, never medical information. If your practice needs a HIPAA-compliant setup, we can connect the page to the secure form or booking tool you already use.

What happens after the two months of changes?
