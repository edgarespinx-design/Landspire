Questions patients ask us

Does getting an implant hurt?The area is numbed, so most patients feel pressure rather than pain. If you're anxious, we offer oral or IV sedation. Afterwards, soreness is usually handled with over-the-counter pain relief.
Am I a candidate?Most adults with healthy gums are. If you've been told you don't have enough bone, there are often options. The free 3D scan tells us for sure.
Does insurance cover implants?Many dental plans cover part of the crown or the procedure. We check your benefits for free and show you exactly what you'd pay.
How long does the whole process take?It depends on your case. At your consult we'll give you a timeline along with your price.
